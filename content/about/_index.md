Audrey Lewis is an actor, singer, and dancer based in Raleigh, North Carolina and Tallahassee, Florida. 

Growing up, Audrey has always had a strong passion for the arts and began taking dance classes at the age of at a very early age. By the age of 8, she began dancing competitively under the direction of CC & Company Dance Complex. Over the years, her training consisted of numerous styles including but not limited to ballet, tap, jazz, contemporary, and hip hop. 

In high school, Audrey decided to step away from the competitive dance world and focus on her love of musical theatre. She participated in several shows at Theatre Raleigh ACT and Broughton High School over the course of her four years. She also had the privilege to receive numerous Superior rankings for her performances at North Carolina Thespian Festival as well as being a two-time Best Actress Finalist at the DPAC Rising Star Awards as well as being titled the 2024 Best Dancer. She also had the honor of performing in the international premiere of _Once Upon a One More Time_ at JTF Atlanta 2026 with Theatre Raleigh ACT.

Although Audrey chose to pursue musical theatre, she is grateful she could continue to grow her dance training throughout high school in her schools dance department. In this program not only was she challenged by the choreography given by guest artists being brought in from NYC and LA but she was also given various opportunities to choreograph and push her creativity in different ways. She truly believes these experiences caused her to be a more versatile artist. 

Audrey is now studying musical theatre at The Florida State University with hopes of getting her BFA in 2030!
